# Technical SEO & High-Converting Copywriting

Strategy, technical checklists, and frameworks for maximizing search engine visibility and user conversions.

## 1. Technical & On-Page SEO
- **Semantic Structure:** Single H1 per page, strict hierarchical H2/H3 nesting, descriptive alt text, and OpenGraph / Twitter card metadata.
- **Search Intent Alignment:** Identify informational, transactional, commercial, or navigational intent; craft title tags (<60 chars) and meta descriptions (<155 chars) that maximize CTR.
- **Schema & Crawlability:** JSON-LD structured data (Article, Product, FAQ, Organization), canonical tags to eliminate duplicate content, and clean URL slugs.

## 2. Direct-Response Conversion Copywriting
- **Persuasive Frameworks:** PAS (Problem - Agitation - Solution), AIDA (Attention - Interest - Desire - Action), and FAB (Features - Advantages - Benefits).
- **Headline Architecture:** Clear over clever. Speak directly to the prospect's primary pain point or desired transformation within the first 3 seconds.
- **Micro-Copy & CTAs:** Frictionless CTA phrasing ("Start Free", "Claim Your Spot"), risk-reversal guarantees, and social proof placement at drop-off points.
