# Data Analytics & Insights Engine

Comprehensive guide for processing, cleaning, analyzing, and deriving actionable intelligence from complex datasets.

## 1. Data Cleaning & Preparation
- **Missing Data Handling:** Mean/median imputation, forward-fill, or record removal depending on distribution and missingness mechanism (MCAR, MAR, MNAR).
- **Outlier Detection:** Z-score thresholding, IQR method, Isolation Forests, and domain-informed boundary clamps.
- **Normalization & Feature Engineering:** MinMax scaling, Standard scaling, log transforms, one-hot encoding, and datetime deconstruction.

## 2. Statistical Analysis & Modeling
- **Hypothesis Testing:** Two-sample t-tests, Mann-Whitney U, Chi-Square, ANOVA, and A/B test power calculation.
- **Correlation & Regression:** Pearson/Spearman coefficients, Ordinary Least Squares (OLS), Logistic Regression, and variance inflation factor (VIF) collinearity checks.

## 3. Metric Design & Visualization
- **KPI Structuring:** Cohort retention, LTV/CAC ratios, DAU/MAU stickiness, churn probability, and funnel drop-off attribution.
- **Visual Delivery:** Produce actionable charts using Plotly, Matplotlib, Seaborn, or structured JSON data feeds for frontend dashboards.
