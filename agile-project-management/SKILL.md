# Technical Project Management & Delivery

Methodology and templates for orchestrating software delivery, managing risk, and maintaining stakeholder alignment.

## 1. Project Planning & Scoping
- **Work Breakdown Structure (WBS):** Decompose milestones into epics, stories, and atomic tasks with clear Definition of Done (DoD).
- **Critical Path Analysis:** Identify dependencies, resource bottlenecks, and slack time across interrelated deliverables.
- **RACI Governance:** Map every milestone to Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed stakeholders.

## 2. Sprint Execution & Cadence
- **Estimation & Capacity:** Use story points / relative sizing. Anchor commitments to historical velocity rather than aspirational deadlines.
- **Risk & Blocker Mitigation:** Maintain an active RAID log (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies) with assigned mitigations.
- **Status Reporting:** Deliver structured executive summaries highlighting blocker triage, milestone trajectory, and upcoming decisions.
